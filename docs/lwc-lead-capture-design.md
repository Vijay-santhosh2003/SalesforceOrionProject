# LWC Lead Capture Design

## Fields to Capture

The LWC will capture the following Lead information:

- First Name
- Last Name
- Company
- Email
- Phone
- State
- Estimated Deal Value
- Marketing Consent

## Brand Colors

The component will follow the specified brand colors:

- **Navy** — used for the header bar.
- **Orange** —

## Component Location

The LWC will be placed on the **Lead App Page**.

This provides a dedicated page where users can enter and submit new Lead information.

## Layout

The component will use a card-style form.

- A navy header bar will appear at the top of the card with the Lead Capture title.
- The form fields will be arranged clearly inside the card.
- Each field will display its validation message inline when the entered value is invalid or required information is missing.
- An orange **Submit** button will appear at the bottom of the form.
- The layout will provide a simple and clear experience for users creating a new Lead.
